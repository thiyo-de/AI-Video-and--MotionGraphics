# Video editing

For video analysis, planning, edits, previews and export checks, follow the [video editing skill](skills/video-editing/SKILL.md). Read only the specialist guidance needed for the requested work.

Keep every project self-contained under `projects/<project-name>/` (`assets/raw/`, `assets/clips/`, `assets/images/`, `audio/`, `manifests/`, `tools/`, and `export/`) — never store project media, scripts, or exports in general root folders.
