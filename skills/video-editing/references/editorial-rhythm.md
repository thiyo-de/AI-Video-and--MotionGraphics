# Editorial Rhythm & Cutting Discipline

## 1. The Word-Per-Minute (WPM) Formula

Never force artificial speed multipliers (`+30%`, `+40%`) onto voiceover. A rushed voiceover destroys authority and signals cheap production.

| Target Video Duration | Total Word Count Budget | Target Speaking Rate | Purpose / Tone |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **15 Seconds** | **24 – 28 words** | 135 WPM | Rapid punchy hook & single CTA |
| **30 Seconds** | **50 – 54 words** | 135 – 140 WPM | Full narrative arc with 3.5s outro hold |
| **60 Seconds** | **110 – 120 words** | 135 – 145 WPM | Explainer / Product walkthrough |

### What to do when a script is too long:
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Speeding up the TTS rate to cram extra sentences.
- ✅ **Required**: Cut redundant words. Delete adjectives, passive verbs, and duplicate points.

## 2. Shot Hold Times & Visual Absorption

Viewers need time to process visual information before new stimuli enter:

| Content Type | Minimum Screen Hold | Why |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Single Word / Bold Hook** | **0.8s – 1.2s** | Instant cognitive punch |
| **Feature Card / Value Pill** | **1.2s – 1.8s** | Time to read icon + title |
| **Complex UI / Screen Tour** | **2.5s – 4.0s** | Eyes need time to scan spatial geometry |
| **Outro Logo & CTA Hold** | **3.0s – 4.5s** | High-status landing; allows music to resolve |

---

## 3. Professional Cut Types

1. **The Match Cut**:
   - Cut between two scenes that share a common visual geometry or motion vector.
   - *Example*: Phone screen displaying a lab -> snap-zoom straight into the physical lab room.
2. **The J-Cut (Audio Lead)**:
   - Next scene's audio or sound effect starts 4 to 8 frames (130ms–260ms) before the visual cut.
   - Creates a subconscious vacuum pulling the viewer into the next frame.
3. **The L-Cut (Audio Hang)**:
   - The dialogue from Scene A continues briefly over the opening frames of Scene B.
   - Smooths transitions during multi-shot facility tours.
4. **Jump Cut on Spoken Emphasis**:
   - Punch-in 110% to 115% scale on key punchy words to re-engage attention without switching assets.
